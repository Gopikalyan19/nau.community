Your Privacy Matters How we protect your data

NAU Community (“NAU”, “we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access our website, programs, events, store, or services.

#Privacy #DataProtection #NAUCommunity
Read Full Privacy Policy
1. About NAU Community

NAU Community is a community-based mental health awareness initiative and operates as a unit of InnerWhispers Wellness LLP.
NAU focuses on awareness, education, and student engagement and does not provide clinical or medical services.

2. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide: Forms, event registrations, contributions, store purchases, or communication via email/social media.
Includes: Name, Email, Phone, Institution, Messages.

b) Payment Information: Processed via secure third-party gateways. NAU does not store card or banking details.

c) Automatically Collected Information: IP address, browser, device info, pages visited for analytics and website improvement.

3. How We Use Your Information

To respond to queries, manage events/programs, process contributions/orders, send updates, and improve services.
We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

Shared only internally for operations or with trusted service providers for delivery.
No sharing for marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Data Protection & Security

We use secure servers, limited access, and trusted platforms.
Note: No method over the internet is completely secure.

6. Cookies

Used to improve functionality and understand user behavior.
You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

Your privacy matters. Read all sections carefully for full details.
