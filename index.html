About NAU

Now, As You Are

Be part of the NAU community
1
A community-first movement

NAU is a youth-driven mental health community creating safe spaces where conversations matter and no one feels alone.

2
Not therapy. Not a clinic.

NAU focuses on connection, listening, and understanding — believing that healing often begins with being heard.

3
Learning, growth & support

Through events, peer support, and learning programs, NAU works to normalize mental health conversations across campuses.

Explore our initiatives
Join the movement
Our Mission

To create safe, stigma-free spaces where students and young adults can talk openly about mental health, feel supported, and grow at their own pace — without pressure or judgment.

Our Vision

A world where mental well-being is treated with the same importance as physical health — and where every student feels seen, heard, and never alone.

Our Core Values

Acceptance over judgment.
Empathy over advice.
Community over isolation.
We believe growth happens when people feel safe to be real — now, as they are.

Inside the Loop – Addiction & Behavioural Patterns

Addressed anxiety, stress, burnout, and emotional struggles through emotional bingo, PHQ/GAD self-assessments, quizzes, and student sharing — creating a safe space for expression and support.
